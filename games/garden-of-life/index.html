Why this garden

Singapore turns super-aged this year. By 2030 nearly one in four citizens will be 65 or older.

Two old practices sit under this game. Reminiscence, which is drawing out the stories a person already carries. And horticultural therapy, tending something living because it does the tender good. Both have been studied in older adults, and both are linked to better mood and steadier wellbeing.

The gains are real and they are small. Nothing here treats anything, and nothing here prevents anything.

Healthy 365 tracks a senior. This game remembers one.

Gardening is not a metaphor either. NParks runs Community in Bloom: more than 2,000 gardening groups and over 48,000 gardeners across the island, with seniors right in the middle of it. A plot on a phone sits beside something plenty of people already do on a Saturday morning.
