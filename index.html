This page is a local signpost, not part of the build. The shipping app is app/, which is also the deploy root on EdgeOne Pages.

Garden of Life Cognitive exercises and a garden that grows from them. Five courses, including the kopitiam table where three kakis play mahjong with you and remember your name.

Open it on a phone, or set your browser to a phone sized viewport before judging anything. The design target is 390×844, portrait.

Round one prototypes are archived, kept as lift-from material: Memory Garden, Mahjong Kakis, Scam Dojo. Mahjong Kakis is the one that got ported into app/; the archived copy is left intact as the reference the port was read from.

The design system lives at design-system/.
